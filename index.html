Crop nutrition

Fertilizer guidance

Understand the general role of common fertilizers before choosing one. The right choice depends on crop, soil test, growth stage and local extension advice.

Balanced nutrients

NPK

Supplies nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P) and potassium (K). Check the nutrient ratio and whether it suits the crop and growth stage.

Best chosen using a soil test.
Nitrogen source

Urea

Mainly supplies nitrogen for leafy growth. It is not a complete fertilizer and should not be applied to every yellowing crop.

Check crop need, timing and local advice.
Starter nutrient source

DAP

Provides phosphorus and nitrogen and is commonly used at planting. Suitability depends on soil and crop needs.

Not suitable for every crop or soil.
